## What is My Track Record?

My Track Record is a monthly report showing your attendance summary — days present, days absent, leave taken, and any salary deductions. You can view but not edit this page.

## What you'll see

| Data | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Month | The reporting period |
| Days Present | Working days you checked in |
| Days Absent | Days marked absent without approved leave |
| Leave Taken | Approved leave days in the month |
| Salary Deduction | Calculated deduction for absences |
| Expected Salary | Your configured salary amount |

## How to view your track record

1. Click **My Track Record** in the sidebar
2. Select a **month** from the dropdown or date picker
3. Review the summary for that month

## Understanding salary deductions

If you had unexcused absences during the month, a salary deduction amount appears in the report. This is calculated based on company rules set by your Super Admin.

> **IMPORTANT:** This is a reference report only. BeetleOps does not process actual payroll — your company's finance team handles salary payments separately.

## Tips

- Review your track record at the end of each month
- If deductions look incorrect, check your attendance logs first, then contact your Super Admin
- Approved leave days should not cause deductions
