## How to apply for leave

As an Employee, you apply for leave from your **Dashboard** — there is no separate menu item for it.

1. **Go to My Dashboard**
   Log in and you land here automatically, or click **My Dashboard** in the sidebar.

2. **Find the Apply for Leave form**
   Scroll down on the dashboard to the leave application section.

3. **Select leave type**
   Choose from available types — Casual, Sick, Unpaid, etc. Your remaining balance for each type is shown.

4. **Pick your dates**
   Select a **Start Date** and **End Date**. The system checks for overlapping requests and insufficient balance.

5. **Add a reason (optional)**
   Type a brief reason for your leave request.

6. **Click Submit**
   Your request is sent to your line manager for approval. You will receive a notification when they decide.

> **IMPORTANT:** You cannot apply for leave if your balance for that type is zero, or if you already have an overlapping pending request.

## After submitting

| Status | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| Pending | Waiting for your manager to approve or reject |
| Approved | Your leave is confirmed — balance is deducted |
| Rejected | Your manager declined — check their comment |
| Cancelled | You or the system cancelled the request |

## What if my request is rejected?

1. Check the notification or your recent requests for the manager's comment
2. You can submit a new request with corrected dates if needed
3. Contact your manager directly if you need clarification
