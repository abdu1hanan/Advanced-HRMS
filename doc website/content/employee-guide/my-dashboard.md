## What is My Dashboard?

My Dashboard is the first page you see after logging in as an Employee. It gives you a quick overview of your leave balances, a form to apply for leave, and your recent leave requests.

## What you'll find here

- **Leave balance cards** — remaining days for each leave type (Casual, Sick, etc.)
- **Apply for Leave form** — submit new leave requests directly from the dashboard
- **Recent requests** — status of your latest leave applications (Pending, Approved, Rejected)

## How to use the dashboard

1. **Check your balances**
   Look at the cards at the top. Each card shows a leave type and how many days you have left this year.

2. **Apply for leave**
   Scroll to the Apply for Leave form. Select your leave type, pick start and end dates, add a reason if needed, and click Submit.

3. **Track recent requests**
   Below the form, see your recent leave requests and their current status. Pending requests are waiting for your manager's approval.

## Who can see this page?

Only **Employees**. Managers and Super Admins have their own dashboards with different information.

## Tips

- Apply for leave as early as possible — your manager needs time to approve
- Check your balances before applying to make sure you have enough days
- If a request shows "Rejected", read the manager's comment for the reason
