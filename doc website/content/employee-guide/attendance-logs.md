## What is this page?

My Attendance & Logs shows your daily attendance history — when you checked in, when you checked out, break times, and session statuses. Data comes from Beetle Sync (Slack) or manual entries by your Super Admin.

## What you'll see

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Date | The working day |
| Check In | Time you signed in |
| Check Out | Time you signed out |
| Status | Active, On Break, Absent, or Off Duty |
| Duration | Total working hours for the day |

## How to view your logs

1. Click **My Attendance & Logs** in the sidebar
2. Browse the table — most recent dates appear first
3. Review your session details for each day

## How attendance gets recorded

- **Slack (Beetle Sync)** — if you send "signin" and "signout" messages in Slack, sessions are recorded automatically
- **Manual (Super Admin)** — your admin may mark absences or adjust records

> **IMPORTANT:** If your attendance looks wrong, ask your Super Admin about submitting a Slack session override (e.g. "signin at 10:00 am").

## Tips

- Check your logs regularly to make sure sessions are recorded correctly
- If you use Slack for attendance, send "signin" when you start and "signout" when you finish
- Absences without approved leave may affect your track record and salary calculations
