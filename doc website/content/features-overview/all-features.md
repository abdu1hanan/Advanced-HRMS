## Core modules explained briefly

**Leave Management** — The full lifecycle of leave: apply, route to manager, approve/reject, track balances and history.

**Attendance & Holidays** — Daily session tracking (check-in, check-out, breaks), absent marking, and company holiday declarations.

**Beetle Sync** — Slack integration that automatically records attendance from chat messages like "signin" and "signout".

**Track Records** — Monthly reports combining attendance data with salary deduction calculations. Exportable to Excel.

**Notifications** — In-app bell alerts and email notifications for leave status changes, override requests, and system events.
