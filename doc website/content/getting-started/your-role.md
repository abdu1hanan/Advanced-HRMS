## The three roles

BeetleOps HRMS uses role-based access control. When you log in, the system checks your role and shows you only the pages and actions you are allowed to use.

## Employee

**Who:** Regular staff members who manage their own leave and attendance.

**Sidebar pages:**
- My Dashboard
- My Attendance & Logs
- My Track Record

**What you can do:**
- View your leave balances and apply for leave from the dashboard
- Check your daily attendance and session logs
- View your monthly track record (read-only)
- Update your profile and password
- Receive notifications about leave status changes

**What you cannot do:**
- Approve or reject other people's leave
- View team members' data
- Change system settings

## Manager (Admin / Line Manager)

**Who:** Team leads and line managers who supervise employees.

**Sidebar pages:**
- Dashboard
- Apply for Leave
- My Attendance & Logs
- Team Directory & Logs
- Track Records
- Pending Leaves
- Leave History

**What you can do:**
- Everything an Employee can do, plus:
- Approve or reject leave requests from your direct reports
- View your team's directory and attendance logs
- See team track records and leave history
- Apply for your own leave via a dedicated page

**What you cannot do:**
- Manage users, departments, or leave types
- Configure company-wide settings or holidays
- Approve Slack session overrides

## Super Admin

**Who:** HR administrators and system owners with full access.

**Sidebar pages:**
- Dashboard, User Management, Departments
- Leave Types, Leave Settings, All Leave Requests, Leave History
- Attendance & Holidays (Attendance Management, Holiday Management)
- Session Management (Beetle Sync, Slack User Mapping, Employee Track Record)

**What you can do:**
- Everything — create users, set roles, configure leave policies
- Declare company holidays and manage attendance
- Approve Slack session overrides and manage user mappings
- View and export all track records

## How is your manager assigned?

When a Super Admin creates your account, they assign a **Line Manager** (your direct boss). Leave requests from you automatically go to that manager for approval. The manager relationship is also used to determine who appears in **Team Directory**.

## Quick comparison

| Feature | Employee | Manager | Super Admin |
|---------|----------|---------|-------------|
| Apply for leave | Yes (dashboard) | Yes (dedicated page) | Yes |
| Approve team leave | No | Yes | Yes (all users) |
| View team data | No | Yes | Yes |
| Manage users | No | No | Yes |
| Configure leave types | No | No | Yes |
| Manage holidays | No | No | Yes |
| Beetle Sync overrides | No | No | Yes |
