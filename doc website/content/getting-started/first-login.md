## Before you start

Make sure your Super Admin has created your account and shared your login email and password. You need a modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari).

## How to log in

1. **Open the HRMS website**
   Go to your company's BeetleOps HRMS URL in your browser. The login page will appear.

2. **Enter your credentials**
   Type your company email in the **Email** field and your password in the **Password** field.

3. **Click Sign In**
   Press the login button. If your credentials are correct, you will be redirected automatically.

4. **Land on your dashboard**
   After login, the system sends you to the dashboard that matches your role — Employee, Manager, or Super Admin.

> **IMPORTANT:** If you forget your password, use the **Forgot Password** link on the login page. Your Super Admin can also reset your password from User Management.

## What you see after login

| Role | Dashboard you land on |
|------|----------------------|
| Employee | My Dashboard |
| Manager (Admin) | Manager Dashboard |
| Super Admin | Admin Dashboard |

Each dashboard shows information relevant to your role — leave balances, pending items, team stats, or system-wide overview.

## First-time checklist

1. Confirm your **name and email** are correct (click your profile in the sidebar)
2. Check your **leave balances** on the dashboard
3. Explore the **sidebar menu** — it shows every page you can access
4. Click the **bell icon** in the top bar to see if you have any notifications
