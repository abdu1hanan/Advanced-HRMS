## The layout at a glance

Every page in BeetleOps HRMS follows the same layout:

- **Top bar** — logo, HRMS title, notification bell, and your name
- **Left sidebar** — navigation menu (different for each role)
- **Main content area** — the page you are viewing
- **Bottom of sidebar** — Profile and Logout links (all roles)

## Top bar

The top bar stays visible on every page.

| Element | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Logo (left)** | Click to collapse or expand the sidebar |
| **HRMS (center)** | App title — always shows "HRMS" |
| **Bell icon (right)** | Opens your notification dropdown |
| **User badge (right)** | Shows your first initial and name |

## Left sidebar

The sidebar is your main navigation. It lists every page you can access based on your role.

- **Active page** is highlighted with a darker background
- **Collapsible groups** (Super Admin only) — click "Attendance & Holidays" or "Session Management" to expand sub-items
- **Profile** and **Logout** appear at the bottom of every sidebar

## Main content area

This is where the actual page content appears — dashboards, tables, forms, and reports. Each page has a title at the top and action buttons (like "Apply", "Approve", or "Export") where relevant.

## Notifications

The bell icon in the top bar shows a red dot when you have unread notifications. Click it to see recent alerts — leave approvals, rejections, override requests, and more. You can filter by **All** or **Unread**, and click **See all** to open the full activity log.

## Profile and logout

At the bottom of the sidebar:

- **Profile** — update your name, email, and password
- **Logout** — sign out of the system securely

## Tips for getting around

1. **Logo click** — toggle the sidebar if you need more screen space
2. **Dashboard first** — always start here; it summarizes what needs your attention
3. **Bell icon** — check it daily for pending approvals or status updates
4. **Sidebar items** — only pages you have permission to see will appear
