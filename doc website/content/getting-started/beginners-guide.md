## Welcome to BeetleOps

If you have never used BeetleOps HRMS before, you are in the right place. This page tells you exactly what to read and in what order, so you never feel lost.

## Recommended reading order

1. **Introduction** — understand what BeetleOps is
2. **First Login** — log in and see your dashboard
3. **Your Role Explained** — find out what you can and cannot do
4. **App Overview** — tour the sidebar, top bar, and notifications
5. **Site Map** — see every page in the app at a glance
6. **Navigation Flows** — step-by-step paths to reach each feature
7. **Your role's guide** — jump to Employee, Manager, or Super Admin docs

## What each section covers

### Getting Started
The basics — what the app is, how to log in, and what your role means.

### Navigating the App
Where everything lives. Includes the full site map and "how to reach X" flows for every feature.

### Features Overview
A plain-language explanation of leave management, attendance, Beetle Sync, track records, and notifications — before you dive into role-specific details.

### Employee / Manager / Super Admin Guide
Detailed walkthroughs for every page in your sidebar. Each page explains what it is, who can use it, and how to use it step by step.

### Reference
FAQ, glossary, permission matrix, and workflow diagrams for when you need a quick answer.

## Quick tips for new users

> **IMPORTANT:** Your sidebar menu is different depending on your role. An Employee sees 3 items; a Manager sees 7; a Super Admin sees many more including collapsible groups.

- Use **Ctrl+K** on this docs site to search for any topic instantly
- The green **notification bell** in the HRMS top bar alerts you when something needs your attention
- Employees apply for leave from the **Dashboard** — there is no separate "Apply for Leave" menu item
- Managers have a dedicated **Apply for Leave** page for their own requests
