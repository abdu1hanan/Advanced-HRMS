## Entry point

When you visit the HRMS URL:

- **Not logged in** → Login page
- **Logged in** → Your role-specific dashboard

The system automatically redirects you based on your role after login.

## Shared pages (all roles)

These pages are available to everyone, regardless of role:

| Page | How to reach it |
|------|----------------|
| Profile | Sidebar → Profile (bottom) |
| Notifications | Top bar → Bell icon |
| Activity Log | Bell dropdown → See all |

## Role-specific pages

Each role sees a different sidebar. If you do not see a menu item, it means your role does not have access to that page. See **Your Role Explained** for details.
