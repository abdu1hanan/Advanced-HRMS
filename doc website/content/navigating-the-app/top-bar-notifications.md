## Top bar elements

The top bar is the horizontal strip at the very top of every page. It contains four key elements:

| Element | Location | Purpose |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Company logo | Left | Click to toggle sidebar open/closed |
| HRMS title | Center | App name display |
| Notification bell | Right | View recent alerts |
| User badge | Right | Shows your initial and name |

## Notification bell

The bell icon opens a dropdown with your recent notifications.

### What triggers notifications?

- Your leave request was **approved** or **rejected**
- A team member submitted a **new leave request** (Managers)
- A **Slack session override** needs approval (Super Admin)
- Any other system event relevant to your role

### Using the notification dropdown

1. **Click the bell icon** in the top bar
2. A red dot appears when you have unread notifications
3. Use filter pills: **All** or **Unread**
4. Click a notification to see details
5. Click **Mark all read** (checkmark icon) to clear unread status
6. Click **See all** to open the full **Activity Log** page

## Activity log

The activity log is a full-page view of all your notifications and system events.

**How to reach it:** Bell dropdown → **See all**

Here you can browse your complete notification history, filter by read/unread status, and review past events.

## User badge

The circular badge on the far right shows:

- Your **first initial** in a colored circle
- Your **full name** (on wider screens)

This is a display-only element — to edit your profile, use **Profile** in the sidebar.

## Tips

- Check the bell daily — pending leave approvals and override requests need timely action
- The red dot only appears for **unread** items; once you open the dropdown, it may clear
- Managers should check notifications whenever a team member applies for leave
