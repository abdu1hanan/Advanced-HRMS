## How to use this page

Each section below shows the exact clicks needed to reach a feature. Follow the steps in order.

---

## Employee flows

### How to apply for leave (Employee)

1. **Log in** to BeetleOps HRMS
2. You land on **My Dashboard** automatically
3. Scroll to the **Apply for Leave** form on the dashboard
4. Select leave type, start date, and end date
5. Click **Submit** — your request goes to your line manager

### How to check leave balance (Employee)

1. Log in → **My Dashboard**
2. View the leave balance cards at the top of the dashboard

### How to view attendance logs (Employee)

1. Log in → click **My Attendance & Logs** in the sidebar
2. Browse your daily session history

### How to view track record (Employee)

1. Log in → click **My Track Record** in the sidebar
2. Select a month to view your attendance summary

## Manager flows

### How to approve a team leave (Manager)

1. Log in → **Dashboard** (check for pending count)
2. Click **Pending Leaves** in the sidebar
3. Find the request → click **Approve** or **Reject**
4. Optionally add a comment before confirming

### How to view team attendance (Manager)

1. Log in → click **Team Directory & Logs** in the sidebar
2. Select a team member to view their attendance logs

### How to apply for your own leave (Manager)

1. Log in → click **Apply for Leave** in the sidebar
2. Fill in the form and submit — goes to your line manager

### How to check team track records (Manager)

1. Log in → click **Track Records** in the sidebar
2. View monthly reports for your team members

---

## Super Admin flows

### How to create a new user (Super Admin)

1. Log in → click **User Management** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add User** or the create button
3. Fill in name, email, role, department, and manager
4. Save — the new user can now log in

### How to declare a company holiday (Super Admin)

1. Log in → expand **Attendance & Holidays** in the sidebar
2. Click **Holiday Management**
3. Add the holiday date and name
4. Save — attendance calculations update automatically

### How to approve a Slack session override (Super Admin)

1. Log in → expand **Session Management** in the sidebar
2. Click **Beetle Sync**
3. Review pending override requests
4. Approve or reject each one

### How to configure leave types (Super Admin)

1. Log in → click **Leave Types** in the sidebar
2. Create or edit leave types (Casual, Sick, Unpaid, etc.)
3. Set rules and save

### How to export track records (Super Admin)

1. Log in → expand **Session Management**
2. Click **Employee Track Record**
3. Select employee and month
4. Click **Export** to download Excel file

---

## Shared flows (all roles)

### How to update your profile

1. Click **Profile** at the bottom of the sidebar
2. Edit your name or email → Save
3. Use the password section to change your password

### How to check notifications

1. Click the **bell icon** in the top bar
2. Browse recent alerts
3. Click **See all** for the full activity log

### How to log out

1. Click **Logout** at the bottom of the sidebar
2. You are returned to the login page
