## What is the Profile page?

The Profile page lets you manage your personal account settings — your name, email address, and password. Every role has access to this page.

## How to reach Profile

1. Look at the **bottom of the left sidebar**
2. Click **Profile**
3. The profile edit page opens

## Update your information

1. **Open Profile** from the sidebar
2. Edit your **Name** or **Email** in the form fields
3. Click **Save** to apply changes

> **IMPORTANT:** If you change your email, use the new email address the next time you log in. Your Super Admin can also update your account from User Management.

## Change your password

1. Open **Profile** from the sidebar
2. Scroll to the **Update Password** section
3. Enter your **Current Password**
4. Enter your **New Password** and confirm it
5. Click **Save**

Use a strong password with at least 8 characters. If you forget your password, use **Forgot Password** on the login page.

## Delete your account

The profile page may include a **Delete Account** option. This permanently removes your account and cannot be undone. Contact your Super Admin before deleting if you are unsure.

## Log out

1. Click **Logout** at the bottom of the sidebar (below Profile)
2. You are signed out and returned to the login page
3. Your session is cleared — you must log in again to access the app

## Security tips

- Never share your password with colleagues
- Log out when using a shared computer
- Change your password immediately if you suspect it has been compromised
- Contact your Super Admin if you are locked out
