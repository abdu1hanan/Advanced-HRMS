## What is the sidebar?

The sidebar is the vertical menu on the left side of every page. It lists all the pages you can access based on your role. It stays visible as you navigate, so you can jump to any section at any time.

## How it works

- **Click any item** to go to that page
- The **currently active page** is highlighted with a darker background
- **Profile** and **Logout** are always at the bottom
- The sidebar can be **collapsed** by clicking the logo in the top bar (gives you more screen space)

## Employee sidebar (3 items)

| Menu item | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| My Dashboard | Home page — leave balances, apply for leave, recent requests |
| My Attendance & Logs | Your daily check-in/out history |
| My Track Record | Monthly attendance and salary summary |

## Manager sidebar (7 items)

| Menu item | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Dashboard | Overview — team stats and pending items |
| Apply for Leave | Submit your own leave requests |
| My Attendance & Logs | Your personal attendance history |
| Team Directory & Logs | View subordinates and their attendance |
| Track Records | Team monthly attendance reports |
| Pending Leaves | Approve or reject team leave requests |
| Leave History | Past team leave decisions |

## Super Admin sidebar (with collapsible groups)

| Menu item | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Dashboard | System-wide overview |
| User Management | Create and edit users |
| Departments | Organization structure |
| Leave Types | Configure leave categories |
| Leave Settings | Company-wide entitlements |
| All Leave Requests | View and manage all leave |
| Leave History | Full audit trail |
| **Attendance & Holidays** ▼ | Collapsible group |
| → Attendance Management | Mark absents, manage logs |
| → Holiday Management | Declare company holidays |
| **Session Management** ▼ | Collapsible group |
| → Beetle Sync | Slack override approval queue |
| → Slack User Mapping | Link Slack to HRMS accounts |
| → Employee Track Record | Full track record management |

## Collapsible groups (Super Admin only)

Two sections have sub-menus that expand and collapse:

1. **Attendance & Holidays** — click the title to expand and see Attendance Management and Holiday Management
2. **Session Management** — click to expand and see Beetle Sync, Slack User Mapping, and Employee Track Record

The group automatically expands when you are on one of its sub-pages.
