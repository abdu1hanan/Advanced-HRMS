## What is this page?

Team Directory & Logs shows all employees who report to you (your direct reports). You can view each team member's profile and drill into their attendance logs.

## What you'll find

- **Team member list** — names and basic info for your subordinates
- **Attendance logs** — click a team member to view their daily session history
- **Quick overview** — see who is present, absent, or on leave

## How to use it

1. **Click Team Directory & Logs** in the sidebar
2. **Browse your team list** — only employees assigned to you as their manager appear
3. **Click a team member** to view their attendance logs
4. **Review session details** — check-in/out times, statuses, and duration

## Who appears in your team?

Employees whose **Line Manager** is set to you in User Management. If someone is missing, ask your Super Admin to verify the manager assignment.

## Tips

- Use this page to spot attendance patterns in your team
- Cross-reference with **Pending Leaves** if someone is absent
- You cannot edit team members' attendance — contact Super Admin for corrections
