## What is this page?

This page shows **your own** attendance history — the same view an Employee sees for themselves. It does not show your team's attendance (use **Team Directory & Logs** for that).

## What you'll see

Daily session records including check-in time, check-out time, status (Active, On Break, Absent, Off Duty), and total duration.

## How to view your logs

1. Click **My Attendance & Logs** in the sidebar
2. Browse your daily session history
3. Verify your sessions are recorded correctly

## Tips

- If you use Slack for attendance, send "signin" and "signout" messages daily
- Contact your Super Admin if sessions are missing or incorrect
- For team attendance, go to **Team Directory & Logs** instead
