## What is the Manager Dashboard?

The Manager Dashboard is your home page after logging in as an Admin / Line Manager. It shows an overview of your team's status — pending leave requests, team attendance summary, and your own leave balances.

## What you'll find here

- **Pending leave count** — how many team requests need your approval
- **Team overview** — summary stats for your direct reports
- **Your leave balances** — your own remaining leave days
- **Quick links** — shortcuts to Pending Leaves and Team Directory

## What to check daily

1. **Pending leave requests** — approve or reject promptly so your team can plan
2. **Team attendance** — spot any unexpected absences
3. **Your own balances** — if you need time off, use **Apply for Leave** in the sidebar

## Who can see this page?

Only **Managers (Admin / Line Manager role)**. The dashboard shows data for your direct reports — employees assigned to you as their line manager.

## Related pages
