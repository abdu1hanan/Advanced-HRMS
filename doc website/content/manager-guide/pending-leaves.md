## What is this page?

Pending Leaves shows all leave requests from your direct reports that are waiting for your decision. This is one of the most important pages for Managers — check it regularly.

## How to approve or reject a leave

1. **Click Pending Leaves** in the sidebar
2. **Review the request** — employee name, leave type, dates, and reason
3. **Click Approve** to accept the leave request
4. **Click Reject** to decline — add a comment explaining why
5. The employee receives a **notification** with your decision

> **IMPORTANT:** Approved leave automatically deducts days from the employee's balance. Rejected leave does not affect their balance.

## What you'll see

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Employee | Who submitted the request |
| Leave Type | Casual, Sick, Unpaid, etc. |
| Dates | Start and end date of leave |
| Days | Number of leave days requested |
| Reason | Employee's explanation |
| Status | Pending (awaiting your action) |

## Best practices

- Respond to requests within 1-2 business days
- Always add a comment when rejecting — explain the reason
- Check for team coverage before approving long leave periods
- If unsure, discuss with the employee before deciding
