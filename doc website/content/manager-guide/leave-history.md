## What is this page?

Leave History shows all past leave requests for your team — not just pending ones. Use it to review previous decisions, audit team leave patterns, and reference past approvals.

## What you'll see

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Employee | Team member who requested leave |
| Leave Type | Category of leave |
| Dates | Start and end dates |
| Status | Approved, Rejected, or Cancelled |
| Decision Date | When the request was resolved |

## How to use it

1. **Click Leave History** in the sidebar
2. **Browse past requests** — filter or search if available
3. **Review decisions** — see what was approved or rejected and when

## When to use this page

- End-of-month review of team leave usage
- Checking if an employee already took leave during a specific period
- Auditing your approval decisions
