## What is this page?

Track Records shows monthly attendance summaries for your team — days present, absent, leave taken, and salary deductions. As a Manager, you can view but not edit these reports.

## What you'll see

For each team member and month:

| Data | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Days Present | Working days checked in |
| Days Absent | Unexcused absences |
| Leave Taken | Approved leave days |
| Salary Deduction | Calculated deduction amount |

## How to use it

1. **Click Track Records** in the sidebar
2. **Select a team member** from the list
3. **Choose a month** to view their report
4. **Review the summary** for attendance and deduction details

## Tips

- Review track records at month-end for your whole team
- Cross-reference with **Team Directory & Logs** if numbers look off
- Contact Super Admin if corrections are needed — Managers cannot edit track records
