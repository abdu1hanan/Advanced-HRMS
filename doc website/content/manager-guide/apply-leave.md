## What is this page?

As a Manager, you have a dedicated **Apply for Leave** page in your sidebar (unlike Employees who apply from the dashboard). Use this page to submit your own leave requests, which go to your line manager for approval.

## How to apply for leave

1. **Click Apply for Leave** in the sidebar
2. **Select leave type** — Casual, Sick, Unpaid, etc.
3. **Pick start and end dates**
4. **Add a reason** (optional)
5. **Click Submit** — your request goes to your line manager

> **IMPORTANT:** Even though you approve your team's leave, your own requests must be approved by your manager (or Super Admin if you report to them).

## After submitting

You will receive a notification when your request is approved or rejected. Check the bell icon in the top bar for updates.

## Tips

- Apply early — your manager needs time to arrange coverage
- Check your leave balances before applying
- If rejected, read the comment and adjust your dates if needed
