## What is Attendance Management?

Attendance Management lets Super Admins view and manage daily attendance for all employees — mark absences, convert absents to approved leave, flag remote work, and review session logs.

## How to reach this page

1. Expand **Attendance & Holidays** in the sidebar
2. Click **Attendance Management**

## Key actions

| Action | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| Mark Absent | Flag an employee as absent for a day |
| Convert to Leave | Change an absence to an approved leave request |
| Remote Work | Mark an employee as working remotely |
| View Logs | Review detailed session history for any employee |

## How to mark an absent

1. Go to **Attendance Management**
2. Select the **date** and **employee**
3. Click **Mark Absent**
4. The absence is recorded and may affect track records

## How to convert absent to leave

1. Find the absent record in the attendance list
2. Click **Convert to Leave**
3. Select the leave type
4. The absence becomes an approved leave — balance is deducted

## Tips

- Review attendance daily to catch unrecorded absences
- Convert absents to leave promptly to avoid incorrect salary deductions
- Cross-reference with Beetle Sync data — Slack may have already recorded sessions
