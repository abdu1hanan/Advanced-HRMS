## What is this page?

Leave History provides a complete record of every leave request ever submitted — approved, rejected, cancelled, or pending. Super Admins can see all users' history, unlike Managers who only see their team.

## What you'll see

- Every leave request in the system
- Employee name, leave type, dates, and final status
- Decision date and any comments from the approver

## How to use it

1. **Click Leave History** in the sidebar
2. **Browse or filter** the full history
3. **Audit decisions** — verify approvals and rejections are correct

## Use cases

- End-of-year leave audit across the organization
- Investigating a specific employee's leave pattern
- Generating reports for HR or compliance
- Verifying manager approval behavior
