## What is Departments?

The Departments page lets you create and manage your company's organizational structure. Each user is assigned to a department, and departments can have designated heads.

## How to create a department

1. **Click Departments** in the sidebar
2. **Click Add Department**
3. **Enter the department name** (e.g. Engineering, HR, Sales)
4. **Assign a department head** (optional) — usually a Manager
5. **Save**

## How departments are used

- Users are assigned to departments during account creation
- Department heads may appear in hierarchy views
- Departments help organize Team Directory and reporting

## Tips

- Keep department names clear and consistent
- Assign department heads who are active Managers
- Review department assignments when employees change roles
