## What is Holiday Management?

Holiday Management lets Super Admins declare official company holidays. When a holiday is set, attendance metrics and leave calculations automatically adjust for that date.

## How to reach this page

1. Expand **Attendance & Holidays** in the sidebar
2. Click **Holiday Management**

## How to declare a holiday

1. **Click Holiday Management**
2. **Click Add Holiday**
3. **Enter the date** and **holiday name** (e.g. "New Year's Day")
4. **Save** — the system applies the holiday across all employees

## What happens when you declare a holiday

- Employees are **not marked absent** on that date
- Attendance calculations skip the holiday
- Leave requests spanning a holiday may count fewer working days
- Track records reflect the holiday in monthly summaries

## Tips

- Declare holidays at the start of the year for the full calendar
- Include both public holidays and company-specific days off
- Review the holiday list before each month to ensure accuracy
- Removing a holiday recalculates affected attendance records
